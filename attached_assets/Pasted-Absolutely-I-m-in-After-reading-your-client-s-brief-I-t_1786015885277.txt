## Here's how we'll work

Rather than trying to squeeze hundreds of lines of code into chat (which is difficult to manage), we'll build it step by step:

1. **Homepage (`index.html`)** – complete.
2. **Gallery (`gallery.html`)** – organized using his project photos.
3. **Contact page (`contact.html`)**.
4. **Shared `style.css`** with the black, orange, and white theme.
5. **`script.js`** for the mobile menu and interactions.

By the end, you'll have a clean project folder that you can drop straight into Replit and publish. I can guide you through each file until the site is complete.
