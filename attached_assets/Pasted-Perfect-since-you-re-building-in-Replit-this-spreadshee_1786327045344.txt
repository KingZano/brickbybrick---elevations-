## Why this matters for your Replit build

This sheet maps **directly** to the Firestore `photos` collection structure, so when you get to the admin panel/upload feature, each row = one document:

```js
{
  category: "Tiling",
  filename: "tiling_bathroom_01.jpg",
  caption: "Bathroom floor & wall tiling",
  featured: false,
  beforeAfter: null,
  imageUrl: "" // filled in once uploaded to Firebase Storage
}
```

So really, you're doing two jobs at once:
1. Sorting/renaming photos on your computer
2. Pre-writing captions & metadata so uploading later is just "browse → pick photo → paste caption → save" — no thinking required mid-upload.
